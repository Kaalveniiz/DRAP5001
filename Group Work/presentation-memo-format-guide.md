## Purpose and document order

One Markdown memo supports three activities: group members build the slides, Shine practises the delivery, and the group consults detailed explanations when preparing or answering questions.

After the document metadata and a short usage note, organise the memo into three main sections:

1. **Slide content** — all slide text and tables, in presentation order.
2. **Speaking cues** — all talking drafts, in the same slide order.
3. **Preparation notes — reference only** — detailed explanations, sources, and working decisions, collected after all slide content and speaking cues.

Finish with the next move and revision history. The presentation brief and drafting decisions belong in the preparation notes so they do not interrupt the material used to build or deliver the slides.

## 1. Slide content

Give each slide a heading such as `### Slide 4 — QMS Processes (Lifecycle)`. Include its title, concise text or table, any needed visual instructions, and a short source footer. Group members should be able to identify exactly what to transfer into the deck.

For the current QMS overview, use:

| QMS Element | Explanation | Short example — atorvastatin tablets |
|:------------|:------------|:-------------------------------------|
| Name of the process | Its general purpose or responsibility | A concrete application to our tablet |

The explanation and example have different jobs: one defines the process; the other shows how it applies to the case. Use the layout appropriate to each later slide rather than imposing this table on every topic.

## 2. Speaking cues

Repeat each slide number and title. Write concise spoken explanations that follow the slide's row or point order and explain the reasoning, rather than simply reading the table aloud.

Use short paragraphs or compact bullets. When using bullets for individual rows, begin with the exact row name in bold so the speaker can match them quickly. Include an opening or transition where useful. Keep lengthy definitions, investigation steps, and source discussion in the preparation notes.

## 3. Preparation notes — reference only

Group the detailed material by slide, then by element in the same order as the slide. Keep each topic's explanation together, including:

- Full definitions and distinctions.
- Worked examples and step-by-step reasoning.
- Assumptions, evidence limits, and answers to likely questions.
- Source links with exact sections, pages, or other verified locations.
- Agreed decisions and points still awaiting discussion.

Preserve useful detail here when shortening the slide or speaking draft. Clearly distinguish hypothetical examples from actual findings. Retain supplied example slides as labelled format references; make clear when a slide still awaits adaptation to our company and product.

## Working method

Discuss one slide at a time. Once wording is agreed, keep the slide text, speaking cues, and detailed notes consistent. If rows are reordered, use the same order in all three sections. Mark provisional numbering and unfinished content clearly.

Maintain the document's dates, version, status, and revision history under [[../../init/working-protocol|the working protocol]]. Approval of the structure does not establish approval of every slide's content.
