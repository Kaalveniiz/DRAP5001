## Product-description recheck

- **Correct:** Our draft's generic-product concept, active ingredient, 20 mg strength, oral route and immediate-release film-coated dosage form match the PDF.
- **Incomplete:** The draft omitted the 30-tablet pack, home-use setting, explicit lipid-lowering endpoints and the group's intended familial-disease population aged ten and above.
- **Partially correct — target-population wording:** The slide's “familial hyperlipidaemia” is too broad for precise indication wording. The US Lipitor label, §1, specifies heterozygous familial hypercholesterolaemia in adults and children ≥10 as an adjunct to diet; homozygous familial hypercholesterolaemia has separate treatment conditions. The report should specify the intended subtype(s) and regional label basis. [DailyMed prescribing information, §§1–2.1](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=a60cc18b-0631-4cf0-b021-9f52224ece65), checked 2026-11-09.
- **Correct — food wording:** “With or without meals” on PDF page 4 agrees with §2.1 of that label. Use this instead of page 3's potentially ambiguous “before and after meals.”
- **Company corrections:** Add the UK to the previously recorded US/EU/China markets and replace the unnamed provisional company with the supplied LipidArc profile.
- **Earlier targets remain separately sourced:** The four-page PDF contains no shelf-life, storage-temperature or bioequivalence target. The existing 24-month / 20–25°C and bioequivalence targets come from [[2026-09-29-presentation-speaking-points#Slide 4 — current table supplied by Shine]], not this PDF. They remain development targets.
- **Source limits:** The PDF supplies no final quantitative formulation, named reference comparator, complete manufacturing instructions or supporting experimental results. Its pack illustration does not establish the actual packaging barrier material or protective performance.
