## Group Paper and Presentation

**Design a Quality Management System for a Medical Device or a Pharmaceutical Product Development**

## Assignment Instructions

**Slide Deck Due: Monday, November 16, 2026 at Midnight**

**Written Report Due: Monday, November 23, 2026 at Midnight**

**Presentation to Class:**

- **Tuesday, November 17 (Medical Device), 2026**
- **Tuesday, November 24 (Drug), 2026**

**For your final presentation and group report, please continue to modify your product from the Case Exercises on its required QMS with QbD.**

Option A – A Fictional product: You may invent a new product (e.g., a novel biologic, a new drug-device combination, or an innovative medical device). This allows you to be creative and design the QbD/QMS system from scratch.

Option B – An Existing commercial product: You may select a marketed product (e.g., a well-known drug or device) and research its publicly available information (e.g., FDA labels, published CMC data, regulatory reviews) to build your quality system around it. However, you will need to explain all the details during the slide presentation. This is not a cut & paste exercise, but rather a reflection of your own learning & understanding of each product attributes.

The learning objective is to demonstrate your ability to apply QbD and QMS principles in a real-world product scenario. The written summary and PPT slides presentation must incorporate all the elements and requirements from the 3 stages of the Case Study product development, published previously in Modules.

## Format

### Written Document

- Your document should be a minimum of 7 pages written narrative, typed, and no more than 10 pages with figures, tables, or images if desired.
- Please put your group member’s names, the date, and the title of your Imaginary Company and Imaginary Product at the top of the first page.
- Please use 1.15 paragraph line spacing and no smaller than 11 font; no larger than 12 font.
- Use 1-inch margins.
- Use a number format for references. References should appear at the end and do not count toward the 5-page minimum written length requirement.
- Use page numbers (in whatever format and location you like).
- Use complete sentences and SYNTHESIZE the information – this document should NOT be pages of bulleted lists or regurgitation of readings. Do NOT copy and paste from FDA or other websites or guidance documents.
- **PLEASE PUT EVERY GROUP MEMBER’S NAME AND THE DATE ON THE DOCUMENT.**
- **EACH TEAM MUST TURN IN One DOCUMENT IN CANVAS TO GET CREDIT.**
- **When turning in the assignment, each student will be asked to affirm that you contributed substantively to the content of the assignment.**

### Slide Deck

- Your slide deck should be around 15 to 20 slides. The first slide should include the name of your group, the Imaginary Company Name, and your Imaginary Product. The remaining slides should address all the required QMS infrastructure required for your selected product development.
- For the slides; you may include images, figures, and tables.
- You will have up to 25 minutes to do your presentation.
- **EACH GROUP MEMBER MUST PRESENT PART OF THE SLIDE DECK TO THE CLASS.**
- **EACH TEAM MUST TURN IN One SLIDE DECK IN CANVAS TO GET CREDIT.**
- **When turning in the assignment, each student will be asked to affirm that you contributed substantively to the content of the assignment.**

## Grading

- You will be graded on your GRAMMAR, SPELLING, AND PUNCTUATION! Please proofread your document before you turn it in.
- We look at SIMILARITY SCORES. This shows us how much you copied and pasted into your work. If you have an unusually high similarity score, you will lose a lot of your grade leading to course failure.
- You will be graded on how well you communicate your thoughts, the flow of the text, how well you synthesized the various sources, and whether you achieved the goals of the assignment.
- You will be graded on your presentation, and how well you communicate your project to your peers.
