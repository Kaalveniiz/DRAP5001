## Agreed report framework

The report consists of an introduction, three main parts, and a conclusion, followed by numbered references.

| Part | Main question it answers | Content at framework level |
|:-----|:-------------------------|:---------------------------|
| **Introduction: Company and product** | What are we developing, and what activities does our company undertake? | Establish the atorvastatin product, company and scope. |
| **1. Product quality foundations** | What quality must this product achieve? | Applicable requirements, QTPP, CQAs and their rationale—the foundation developed in the first case study. |
| **2. QbD development and control strategy** | How will we understand and control the factors affecting that quality? | Material and process risks, proposed studies, design-space work and the resulting control strategy—the second case study’s main contribution. |
| **3. QMS implementation and evaluation** | How will the company put those controls into practice and keep them effective? | Responsibilities and lifecycle processes, the quality-manual excerpt, internal audit and follow-up—the third case study’s main contribution. |
| **Conclusion** | How does the combined approach meet the product’s quality objectives? | Draw the system together and acknowledge remaining development needs. |

**References:** Numbered references at the end, cited in the report text.

### Connecting logic

**Define the required quality → develop the controls → establish the organisation and procedures that maintain and evaluate them.**

Each case-study stage has a visible place in the report, with explanations showing how the stages build on one another. Within Part 3, the **QMS processes, quality-manual excerpt and audit checklist** will each receive explicit treatment.
